**This release is all about the asset/map picker in the upload panel.**
Dedicated to @MayorCheeks and his 700-mods playset!

- **The asset dropdown is now searchable.** Open it and start typing: the list filters as you type, matching both the asset name and its author, with the matching letters highlighted. Typos are tolerated, reasonably.
- **The asset dropdown is much faster.** Thanks to a virtual list, only the rows you can actually see are built now, instead of one per mod in your playset, and each row loads a small thumbnail instead of the full-size cover image. Opening the list and typing in it no longer stutters, however large your playset is.
- Fixed the asset dropdown row being the wrong height at any resolution other than 1920 wide; it now matches the description field and follows your font-size setting.
- Fixed an issue with the mod settings being corrupted for at least one user due to an edge-case interaction with SMC+. Affected settings are now restored to their default.
- Updated the Polish translation.
