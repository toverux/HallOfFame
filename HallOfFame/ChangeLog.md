**The screenshot details update!**

Since 2026.0.0 you could attach a description, your photo mode settings and your playset to a screenshot, but nobody could see them yet. Now you can, in a new screenshot details window in the main menu.

- **Screenshot details window.** Press D (rebindable) or click the new details row in the slideshow controls. It has three tabs: Description, Photo mode settings and Playset. An empty tab tells you whether the creator chose not to share it or the screenshot is older than the feature.
- **Read descriptions.** The first line shows in the slideshow controls, the whole text in the window.
- **See the photo mode settings a creator used**, named and grouped as in the game's own Photo Mode, with notes on why copying them does not always reproduce the look.
- **See the conditions a screenshot was taken in**: weather, season, temperature, time of day, latitude and sun height, and the exposure and white balance the game picked on its own. They are recorded from this version on, along with your photo mode settings when you share them.
- **Browse a creator's playset**, with each mod's version and size. Filter it by kind of mod and by asset category, or show only the mods you don't have. Hover a mod to cycle through its preview images. Mods no longer on Paradox Mods are dimmed and listed last.
- **Skyve's verdict on every mod.** The playset shows which mods Skyve marks as stable, outdated or broken, with the reviewer's note in the tooltip. The showcased mod card shows it on hover too.
- **Fixes a game bug.** After Photo Mode is opened once, the game's automatic exposure and white balance stop following the season and the weather for the rest of the session. Hall of Fame now fixes that for the time being.
- Many other small fixes and improvements.
